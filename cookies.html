Cookie Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to the website owners.

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies on this website for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function properly (e.g., language preference)
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website so we can improve it

3. Cookies we use

weweb-lang (essential): Stores your language preference. Duration: persistent. Provider: WeWeb Studio.

If we use analytics tools in the future, we will update this policy and request your consent before placing any non-essential cookies.

4. Managing cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or delete existing ones. Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of this website.

5. Third-party cookies

We do not currently use third-party cookies. If this changes in the future, we will update this policy and inform you accordingly.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this cookie policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about our use of cookies, please contact us at hello@weweb.studio.
