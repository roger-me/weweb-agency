Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Data Controller

WeWeb Studio, based in Barcelona, Spain. Contact: hello@weweb.studio

2. What data we collect

We may collect the following information when you use our website or contact us:

  • Name and email address (when you contact us via the contact form)
  • Browsing data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) collected through cookies
  • Any additional information you voluntarily provide

3. Purpose of data processing

We use your data to respond to your inquiries, provide our services, improve our website, and comply with legal obligations. We will never sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Legal basis

We process your data based on your consent (contact form submissions), our legitimate interest (website analytics), and compliance with legal obligations where applicable.

5. Data retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described above, or as required by law. Contact form data is kept for a maximum of 12 months.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR and Spanish data protection law (LOPDGDD), you have the right to access, rectify, delete, restrict processing, and port your data. You can exercise these rights by emailing us at hello@weweb.studio.

7. Third-party services

We may use third-party services such as hosting providers and analytics tools. These services may process your data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
